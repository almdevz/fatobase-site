Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026.

This policy explains how FatoBase handles information when its publishing workflow is connected to third-party platforms such as TikTok.

1. About FatoBase

FatoBase is an independent digital publishing project that creates, manages and publishes educational short-form documentary content.

2. Information processed

Depending on the platform permissions granted by an authorized account owner, FatoBase may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, publication identifiers, publication status, media metadata and performance metrics required to operate the requested publishing features.

3. TikTok integration

When an authorized TikTok account is connected, FatoBase uses TikTok permissions only for the features approved by the account owner, including content publishing, publication-status tracking and related publishing operations supported by TikTok APIs.

4. Authorization tokens

Access and refresh tokens are used only to authenticate authorized API requests. Tokens are not intentionally exposed to the public and are not sold to third parties.

5. Media and publication data

FatoBase may process video files, captions, publication identifiers and status information in order to publish and monitor FatoBase content. Media may be temporarily made available through controlled HTTPS URLs when required by a platform's publishing API.

6. Sharing of information

FatoBase does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to service providers and third-party platforms only when necessary to perform the publishing, hosting, automation or analytics functions requested by the authorized user.

7. Retention

Operational records such as publication identifiers and status history may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to maintain publication history, troubleshoot integrations and collect authorized performance metrics. Temporary media files may be removed after processing when no longer required.

8. Revoking access

An account owner may revoke permissions through the settings provided by the connected third-party platform. After revocation, FatoBase will no longer be able to use the revoked authorization for future API requests.

9. Data deletion requests

Requests concerning deletion of information associated with the FatoBase service can be submitted through the support channel identified on the Support & Contact page.

10. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce unauthorized access to credentials, tokens and operational data. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

FatoBase is not designed as a service directed to children under 13.

12. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when services, integrations or legal requirements change. The current version will remain publicly available at this URL.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, use the contact channel listed on the FatoBase Support & Contact page.

Resumo em português

O FatoBase utiliza dados e autorizações de plataformas de terceiros somente para executar funcionalidades autorizadas, como publicação de conteúdo, acompanhamento do status da publicação e coleta de métricas permitidas. O FatoBase não vende informações pessoais. O acesso pode ser revogado pelo usuário na plataforma correspondente, e solicitações relacionadas a dados podem ser encaminhadas pela página de suporte.
