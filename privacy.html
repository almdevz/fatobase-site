Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FatoBase collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when you visit this website or authorize a supported third-party publishing integration such as TikTok.

1. Who we are

FatoBase is an independent educational media and digital publishing service focused on producing and publishing short-form documentary content. This website provides public information about the service, its publishing workflow, supported integrations, legal terms and contact channels.

2. Scope of this policy

This Policy applies to information processed through the FatoBase website and through authorized platform integrations used for publishing, publication-status monitoring and related operations. Third-party platforms have their own privacy policies, and their processing is governed by those policies in addition to any applicable platform terms.

3. Information we may collect or process

Depending on how FatoBase is used, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Website and technical data: basic server logs, IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, date/time and diagnostic data required for security and operation.
  • Support information: information voluntarily provided when contacting FatoBase, such as an email address, message content and information needed to answer the request.
  • Platform account data: account or user identifiers made available by a connected platform after authorization.
  • Authorization data: access tokens, refresh tokens, granted scopes and related authorization metadata required to make authorized API requests.
  • Publishing data: video files, captions, titles, media metadata, publication identifiers, publication status and error or diagnostic information.
  • Performance data: metrics or analytics that the connected platform makes available under the permissions granted by the account owner.

4. How we use information

FatoBase uses information only for legitimate service purposes, including:

  • operating and securing the website;
  • authenticating authorized third-party integrations;
  • preparing, transferring and publishing FatoBase-produced content;
  • checking publication status and handling publishing errors;
  • collecting authorized performance metrics where supported;
  • maintaining operational history and troubleshooting integrations;
  • responding to support, privacy and data-deletion requests;
  • complying with applicable legal obligations and platform requirements.

5. TikTok integration and permissions

FatoBase uses TikTok APIs only after the relevant TikTok account owner authorizes the integration and grants the required permissions. The TikTok Content Posting API is used for supported publishing operations involving FatoBase-produced media. TikTok permissions are not used for unrelated purposes.

FatoBase does not sell TikTok account information, authorization data or personal information. Access to TikTok can be revoked by the account owner using the controls provided by TikTok.

6. Legal basis and consent

Where applicable, FatoBase processes information on the basis of user authorization or consent, performance of requested services, legitimate operational and security interests, and compliance with legal obligations. When a platform requires explicit authorization, FatoBase does not attempt to use that platform integration before authorization is granted.

7. Sharing and service providers

FatoBase does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary with infrastructure, hosting, automation, storage, analytics or social-platform providers that support the requested service, or when required by law. Such providers process data under their own terms and privacy obligations.

8. International data transfers

Some service providers or social platforms may process information in countries other than the country where the user is located. Where applicable, FatoBase relies on the safeguards and contractual mechanisms made available by those providers and applicable law.

9. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed, including service operation, security, troubleshooting, publication history, legal compliance and authorized analytics. Temporary media files may be deleted after processing when no longer needed. Authorization data is no longer used after access is revoked or otherwise becomes invalid.

10. Security

FatoBase uses reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to reduce unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or loss of credentials, tokens and operational data. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

The public FatoBase website is intended to provide informational content and may use only technical mechanisms necessary for hosting, security and delivery. If analytics, cookies or similar technologies are added in the future, this Policy will be updated to describe their use.

12. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request confirmation of processing, access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, information about sharing, revocation of consent or authorization, and other rights provided by law. Requests can be submitted through the contact method on the Support & Contact page.

13. Revoking platform access

An account owner may revoke FatoBase permissions using the controls provided by the connected third-party platform. Once access is revoked, FatoBase will no longer use that revoked authorization for future API requests.

14. Data deletion requests

To request deletion of information associated with FatoBase, contact the address listed on the Support & Contact page and use the subject FatoBase Privacy Request. The request may require reasonable verification before deletion is performed. Some limited records may be retained when required for security, fraud prevention or legal compliance.

15. Children

FatoBase is not designed as a service directed to children under 13, and the service does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through this website.

16. Third-party links and services

This website may reference or integrate with third-party services. FatoBase is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of third parties. Users should review the privacy policies of those services.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

FatoBase may update this Policy when the service, integrations, legal requirements or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this same public URL and the “Last updated” date will be revised.

18. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or concerns, visit the FatoBase Support & Contact page or email alan80mendes@gmail.com.
