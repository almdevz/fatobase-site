ABOUT THE SERVICE

A real publishing workflow, not a login or placeholder page

FatoBase operates as an independent educational publishing project. This website publicly describes the service, its editorial workflow, supported integrations, privacy practices, terms and contact channels. No login is required to access these pages.

FatoBase is focused on concise educational storytelling. When a subject includes uncertainty, hypotheses or competing interpretations, the editorial approach is to distinguish those from documented facts.

TIKTOK INTEGRATION

Official Content Posting API integration

FatoBase uses the TikTok Content Posting API to publish FatoBase-produced videos to a TikTok account only after the account owner has authorized the integration and granted the required permissions.

The integration is used for supported publishing operations, media upload or transfer, publication-status tracking and related functions. FatoBase does not sell TikTok account information and does not use TikTok permissions for unrelated purposes.

Authorization can be revoked by the account owner through the controls made available by TikTok. Additional details are available in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
