Support & Contact

This public page provides support and contact information for FatoBase, including platform-integration questions, privacy requests and data-deletion requests.

General contact

Email: alan80mendes@gmail.com

TikTok integration support

If a TikTok authorization is no longer desired, revoke the FatoBase authorization using the controls made available by TikTok. After revocation, the revoked authorization will no longer be used for future API requests.

Privacy and data requests

For access, correction, deletion, revocation or other privacy requests concerning information processed by FatoBase, send an email with the subject FatoBase Privacy Request. Please provide enough information to identify the request without including passwords, access tokens, client secrets or other confidential credentials.

Publishing support

For publishing or integration issues, describe the affected platform, approximate date/time and publication or workflow step. Do not send authentication secrets.

Response and verification

FatoBase may request reasonable verification before acting on a privacy or data-deletion request in order to protect account and data security.
