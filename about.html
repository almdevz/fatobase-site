About FatoBase

FatoBase is an independent educational media and digital publishing service focused on short-form documentaries. Its editorial scope includes science, history, technology, archaeology, engineering, natural phenomena and documented curiosities.

Editorial approach

FatoBase aims to present concise explanations based on documented information. When a subject includes uncertainty, hypotheses or competing interpretations, the content should distinguish those from established facts.

Production workflow

The workflow combines topic selection, research organization, script preparation, narration, visual assembly, captioning, publication preparation, authorized social-platform publishing and performance monitoring.

Service purpose

The service exists to organize and publish FatoBase educational media through supported official platform integrations. The public website provides information about the project, service features, publishing workflow, privacy practices, terms and support channels.

Social publishing

FatoBase uses official platform integrations where available. Connected accounts must authorize the required permissions before content can be published or publication status can be checked.

TikTok

The TikTok integration is used to publish FatoBase-produced videos through the TikTok Content Posting API and to check the status of supported publishing operations. Visibility and public availability remain subject to the controls, moderation and policies provided by TikTok.

Transparency

Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, service information and support details are publicly accessible without authentication from this website.
