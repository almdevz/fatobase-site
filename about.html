About FatoBase

FatoBase is an independent digital publishing project focused on educational short-form documentaries. Its editorial scope includes science, history, technology, archaeology, engineering, natural phenomena and documented curiosities.

Editorial approach

FatoBase aims to present concise explanations based on documented information. When a subject includes uncertainty, hypotheses or competing interpretations, the content should distinguish those from established facts.

Production workflow

The workflow combines topic selection, research organization, script preparation, narration, visual assembly, captioning, publication preparation and performance monitoring.

Social publishing

FatoBase uses official platform integrations where available. Connected accounts must authorize the required permissions before content can be published or publication status can be checked.

TikTok

The TikTok integration is used to publish FatoBase-produced videos through the TikTok Content Posting API and to check the status of those publishing operations. Visibility and public availability remain subject to the options, moderation and policies provided by TikTok.

Transparency

Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are publicly accessible without authentication from every page of this website.
